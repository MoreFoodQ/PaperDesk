## Main changes

- Traditional Chinese localization with terminology commonly used in Taiwan.
- Reading workspace, auxiliary panels, and dark palettes.
- Codex CLI integration, quota display, and local usage management.
- Local authentication, download validation, secret protection, and PDF worker limits.
- Pinned dependency installation, source packaging, and regression coverage.

The project retains the upstream PDF, library, annotation, model, and export foundations. The license is preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE); third-party notices are in [licenses/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](licenses/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
